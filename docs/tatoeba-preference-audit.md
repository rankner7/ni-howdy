# Tatoeba preference translation audit

Reviewed on 2026-11-14 against the bundled 2026-02-13 ManyThings.org export.
Scope: sentences containing 喜歡 and an English answer containing love, loves, loved, or loving. This is an editorial review of this specific pattern, not a quality audit of all 27,121 sentences.

- 38 matching records: 36 ordinary preference uses and 2 other senses.
- 29 ordinary-preference records lack a like-form translation in the original export. The train sentence had already been corrected locally, leaving 28 new grading gaps.
- 7 ordinary-preference records already include a like-form answer.
- All 36 now include a reviewed or imported like-form answer. Original imported answers remain accepted.
- Two different senses remain unchanged: love stories (#818094) and romantic 喜歡上 / in love (#5405489).
- Very/really, subjects, and tense are retained in reviewed answers. The unmarked banana sentence has both present and past like-form alternatives.

Run `.venv/bin/python scripts/audit_preference_translations.py` for machine-readable results, including the original contributor attribution. Corrections are stored in `nihowdy/sentences.py` and apply to existing saved phrases, new phrase candidates, and displayed examples.
